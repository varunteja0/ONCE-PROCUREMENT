DPA summary

How Once processes customer data during a pilot.

This page summarizes the data-processing terms Once expects to include in pilot agreements. It is not a substitute for the signed DPA.

Roles

  • Customer is the controller or business owner of customer data.
  • Once acts as a processor/service provider for data handled on the customer's behalf.
  • Once uses subprocessors only to host, secure, observe, bill, and support the service.

Subprocessors

Provider Purpose Data handled
Fly.io Backend/API hosting Application data and logs
Vercel Frontend/landing hosting Website telemetry and static assets
Cloudflare DNS, edge, Workers/R2/KV where applicable Request metadata and app data for enabled services
Sentry Error monitoring Error context, stack traces, metadata
Stripe Billing Billing contact and payment metadata
Resend Transactional email where enabled Email address and message metadata

Customer rights and assistance

Once assists with reasonable export, correction, deletion, and access requests for customer-controlled data, subject to signed contract terms, legal obligations, and preservation of receipt/audit evidence.

Security commitments

  • Use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards for customer data.
  • Restrict operational access to personnel with a business need.
  • Notify affected customers of confirmed security incidents according to the signed agreement.
  • Maintain a current subprocessor list and provide notice of material changes for contracted customers.
