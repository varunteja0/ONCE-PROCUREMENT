How it works

Three steps. One source of truth.

1

Capture once

Producer fills a single Once profile — licenses, E&O, appointments, NAIC IDs, banking. Stored with tenant-scoped access controls and least-privilege operations.

2

Submit to many

Browser extension and server-side Playwright bots fill carrier portals at machine speed for supported pilot portals, with explicit per-portal consent, captcha-aware blocking, and drift monitoring for automation changes.

3

Prove forever

Every successful submission emits an Ed25519-signed receipt — payload hash, consent ID, timestamp, and portal terms hash when captured. Verifiable by anyone at verify.getonce.com.

Receipts you can verify

Evidence your team can audit.

Every Once receipt is an Ed25519 signature over a canonical JSON payload — supplier, portal, submission time, hash of exactly what was submitted, and the consent that authorized it. Public keys are published. Verification is one HTTP call. No vendor lock-in.

  • ✓ Cryptographically verifiable, not just logged
  • ✓ Per-portal consent ledger, signed by the supplier
  • ✓ Public verifier at verify.getonce.com
  • ✓ Receipt protocol open-sourced (Q2 2027 roadmap)
