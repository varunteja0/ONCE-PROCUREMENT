Privacy policy

We only use customer data to operate the service.

This pilot privacy policy explains how Once Procurement, Inc. handles personal and business information submitted through Once websites, applications, browser extensions, verifier services, and support channels.

Data we process

  • Account data: name, email, role, tenant membership, and authentication events.
  • Producer and supplier data: legal names, licenses, tax identifiers, contact details, documents, and portal-specific fields supplied by the customer.
  • Portal activity: consent records, submission status, screenshots where enabled, receipts, and audit logs.
  • Operational data: device/browser metadata, logs, support messages, billing events, and security telemetry.

How we use data

  • Provide, secure, monitor, and support the Once service.
  • Prepare and submit customer-authorized portal updates.
  • Create signed receipts, audit trails, and customer exports.
  • Investigate abuse, security incidents, reliability issues, and billing disputes.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell customer data.
  • We do not use customer tenant data to train public AI models.
  • We do not submit to a portal without customer authorization in the pilot scope.
  • We do not share one customer tenant's data with another customer tenant.

Retention and deletion

Pilot customer data is retained for the service term and a limited post-termination period needed for audit, security, billing, and legal obligations. Customers can request export or deletion through their contract owner. Signed receipts and audit records may be retained when needed to preserve evidence of authorized submissions.

Requests

Send privacy, export, deletion, or correction requests to privacy@getonce.com. Security reports go to security@getonce.com.
