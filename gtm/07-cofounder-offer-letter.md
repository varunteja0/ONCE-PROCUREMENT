# Cofounder Offer Letter — Template

> **Status:** Template v0.1 — must be reviewed by a Delaware corporate attorney
> before signature. Do not deliver unredlined.

---

**From:** <<FOUNDER_NAME>>, Founder & CEO, Once Procurement, Inc.
**To:** <<CANDIDATE_NAME>>
**Date:** <<OFFER_DATE>>
**Re:** Paid trial → cofounder role at Once Procurement

<<CANDIDATE_NAME>>,

The reason this letter exists is that I want you to help build Once Procurement
into the system of record for insurance procurement. I am writing it before we
make any equity grant because both sides should know exactly what they are
signing up for.

This is a two-stage offer. Stage one is a **60–90 day paid trial**. Stage two
is a **cofounder grant**, contingent on a mutual decision at the end of the
trial.

---

## 1. Stage one — paid trial

- **Trial window:** <<TRIAL_START_DATE>> through <<TRIAL_END_DATE>>
  (target 60 days, extendable to 90 by mutual agreement).
- **Compensation:** USD <<TRIAL_STIPEND_USD>> per month, paid via contractor
  agreement (1099 in the US, equivalent invoice elsewhere). Range is
  $3,000–$5,000/mo depending on scope.
- **Scope:** <<TRIAL_SCOPE_ONE_SENTENCE>>. Concretely we expect two to four
  shipped artifacts (PRs merged, customer calls run, design docs adopted)
  that map back to the current quarter goals.
- **Time commitment:** at least 20 hrs/week, scheduled however you want.
- **Tooling:** GitHub access to the relevant repos, a tenant in
  `demo.once-procurement.app`, and a seat in Slack `#core`. No prod write
  access during the trial.
- **Termination:** either side may end the trial with 7 days' notice. Final
  invoice is paid pro-rata through the last working day.

The trial is not an interview cosplay. The work has to ship.

## 2. Stage two — cofounder grant

If we both want to continue at the end of the trial, you become a cofounder
with the following terms:

- **Title:** <<COFOUNDER_TITLE>> (e.g. Cofounder & CTO / Cofounder & Head of
  Distribution).
- **Equity:** <<EQUITY_PCT>>% of fully-diluted common stock, issued as
  restricted common via a Restricted Stock Purchase Agreement.
  - Domain-expert cofounders (insurance distribution / underwriting):
    typical range **5–8%**.
  - Senior engineering / GTM cofounders joining after first revenue:
    typical range **1.5–3%**.
- **Vesting:** **4-year vest with a 1-year cliff**, measured from
  `<<TRIAL_START_DATE>>` (i.e. the trial period counts toward vesting).
  After the cliff, monthly vesting for the remaining 36 months.
- **83(b) election:** you must file an 83(b) with the IRS within 30 days of
  the stock purchase. We will provide the form and reimburse the filing
  costs. Failing to file is your responsibility.
- **Acceleration:** **double-trigger** — 100% of unvested shares accelerate
  if (a) the company is acquired and (b) you are terminated without cause or
  resign for good reason within 12 months of close.
- **Right of repurchase:** the company retains the right to repurchase
  unvested shares at original purchase price if you leave before fully
  vested.
- **Cash compensation:** USD <<COFOUNDER_BASE_USD>> per year, reviewable at
  the next priced round.

We will sign a Confidential Information and Invention Assignment Agreement
(CIIAA) and a standard cofounder agreement on the same day as the RSPA.

## 3. What I expect from you

- You ship. The cadence is roughly one substantive thing per week — a
  shipped feature, a closed customer, a hired teammate, a fixed-on-its-own
  process. The bar is "this would not have happened without me."
- You disagree out loud. Silent disagreement compounds.
- You take ownership of `<<OWNERSHIP_AREA>>` end-to-end. That means
  problem-finding, not just problem-solving.
- You treat customers, candidates, and investors with the same respect you
  treat me. Public posts and private DMs both count as your reputation and
  the company's.

## 4. What you can expect from me

- Honest weekly 1:1s. No surprises in reviews.
- A board that backs you on hires you believe in.
- Transparent cap table, transparent runway, transparent customer pipeline.
- If we shut down or you leave: a public reference and a clean exit.

## 5. Conversion decision

By <<TRIAL_END_DATE>> we will agree, in writing, on one of three outcomes:

1. **Convert.** Sign the cofounder agreement and RSPA within 14 days.
2. **Extend.** Add up to 30 days to the trial with the same stipend.
3. **End.** Final payment, mutual NDA stays in force.

No outcome is "default." Either of us can pick "End" without explanation.

with redlines — that is a feature, not a problem.
