## MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**This Master Services Agreement** ("Agreement") is entered into as of **[EFFECTIVE_DATE]** ("Effective Date") between:

- **Once Identity, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (in formation; Indian operating entity **[YOUR_INDIAN_ENTITY_NAME]** until US incorporation completes), having its principal place of business at **[YOUR_ADDRESS]** ("**Service Provider**" or "**Once**"); and
- **[CUSTOMER_LEGAL_NAME]**, a **[CUSTOMER_STATE]** **[entity type — corporation / LLC]**, having its principal place of business at **[CUSTOMER_ADDRESS]** ("**Customer**" or "**MGA**").

Each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

### 1. Services

Service Provider will provide Customer with: (a) a managed service to submit Customer's regulatory, financial, insurance, and identity data ("Customer Data") to third-party carrier portals identified by Customer ("Target Portals"); (b) cryptographically signed receipts evidencing each submission; and (c) ongoing monitoring and renewal of time-sensitive Customer Data including producer licenses, errors-and-omissions certificates, certificates of insurance, and W-9 records (collectively, "Services").

### 2. Scope & SLAs

- **Pilot scope:** up to **50** active Target Portals, unlimited submissions during the Term.
- **Customer approval required:** every submission requires Customer's per-submission electronic approval. Service Provider will not submit data to any Target Portal without an approval record signed by an authorized Customer user.
- **Uptime:** Service Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain ≥ 99% monthly uptime of the approval dashboard. No SLA credits during pilot.
- **Support:** email and Slack support, business-hours response (next business day), Eastern Time.

### 3. Fees

- **Setup fee:** USD **$2,500**, due on Effective Date, wired to Service Provider's bank account per Schedule A.
- **Monthly fee:** USD **$1,500**, billed monthly in advance starting on the 30th day after Effective Date.
- **Pilot pricing lock:** the monthly fee is locked at $1,500 for 24 months from Effective Date, regardless of standard list price changes.
- **Out of scope:** any portal beyond 50 active, custom integration work, or onsite engagement is billed at $200/hr with prior written approval.

### 4. Term & Termination

- **Initial Term:** 30 days from Effective Date (pilot). After Initial Term, this Agreement renews **month-to-month**.
- **Termination for convenience:** either Party may terminate on **30 days' written notice** after the Initial Term ends.
- **Termination for cause:** either Party may terminate immediately on material breach uncured for 15 days after written notice.
- **Effect of termination:** Service Provider will export all Customer Data, all signed receipts, and the encryption keys necessary to verify those receipts to Customer within 14 days, then delete its copies within 30 days.

### 5. Data Ownership & Use

- **Customer Data** is and remains the sole property of Customer.
- Service Provider may process Customer Data only to provide the Services. Service Provider will not (a) share Customer Data with any third party other than the Target Portals Customer designates, (b) use Customer Data to train any machine-learning model that is offered to any other customer, or (c) sell, license, or otherwise commercialize Customer Data.
- Service Provider may use **aggregated, de-identified statistics** (e.g., "average submission time across all customers") for product improvement and marketing, provided no Customer is identifiable.

### 6. Confidentiality

Each Party will protect the other Party's Confidential Information with the same standard of care it uses for its own, and at minimum reasonable care. "Confidential Information" includes Customer Data, Service Provider's source code, both Parties' financial terms, and any non-public business information. Obligations survive termination for 3 years.

### 7. Security

Service Provider will: (a) encrypt Customer Data in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256); (b) maintain access controls limiting Customer Data access to personnel with a need to know; (c) maintain SOC 2 Type I within 6 months of Effective Date, and Type II within 18 months; (d) notify Customer of any Security Incident affecting Customer Data within 72 hours of discovery.

### 8. Representations & Warranties

Service Provider warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional, workmanlike manner. EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS" AND SERVICE PROVIDER DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED.

### 9. Indemnification

Service Provider will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Customer from any third-party claim alleging that the Services, as delivered by Service Provider and used as authorized, infringe a US intellectual property right. Customer's exclusive remedy under this section is the foregoing defense and indemnity.

### 10. Limitation of Liability

EXCEPT FOR (A) BREACHES OF CONFIDENTIALITY (SECTION 6), (B) SECURITY OBLIGATIONS (SECTION 7), AND (C) INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS (SECTION 9), NEITHER PARTY'S LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT WILL EXCEED **THE FEES PAID BY CUSTOMER IN THE 12 MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM**. NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR CONSEQUENTIAL, INCIDENTAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.

### 11. No Auto-Submission Without Consent

Service Provider expressly acknowledges that no submission to any Target Portal will occur without Customer's explicit per-submission electronic approval. Service Provider further acknowledges that any submission made in violation of this provision is a material breach.

### 12. Compliance with Third-Party Terms of Service

Service Provider will operate within the terms of service of each Target Portal. Customer represents that Customer has authority to submit Customer Data to each Target Portal. Service Provider will pause or stop service to any Target Portal whose ToS prohibits third-party submission upon discovery.

### 13. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **State of Delaware**, USA, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. Any dispute will be resolved by binding arbitration in **Wilmington, Delaware** under the JAMS Streamlined Arbitration Rules, except either Party may seek injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.

### 14. General

- **Entire agreement:** this Agreement is the complete agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements.
- **Amendment:** written, signed by both Parties.
- **Assignment:** neither Party may assign without the other's prior written consent, except to an affiliate or in connection with a merger / acquisition / sale of substantially all assets.
- **Notices:** by email to the addresses on the signature block.
- **Counterparts / electronic signature:** signatures may be electronic and in counterparts.
